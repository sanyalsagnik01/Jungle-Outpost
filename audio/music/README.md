# Music

The soundtrack. Every track is from OpenGameArt.org and released under **CC0** (public domain): free to use, change and share, with no attribution required. They are credited here anyway. `js/music.js` picks the track for the current situation and crossfades between them. Delete this folder and the game falls back to its synthesized music.

Tracks were trimmed for looping, loudness-normalized and re-encoded to MP3; the originals are linked below.
